STICKER MAKER (AI Expert, Module 5, Lesson 3)

WHAT YOU WILL BUILD
A camera app that cuts out ANYTHING you point at. Press SPACE to freeze the picture, click an object, and the AI
outlines it in yellow. Press s and it is saved as a sticker: a PNG picture of only that object, with a see-through
background, ready to drop into a chat, a slide or a poster.

THE AI
SAM (Segment Anything Model 2.1, tiny), made by Meta AI. It comes in two parts that run on your own computer:
part 1, the encoder, studies the frozen photo once (about a second); part 2, the decoder, turns every click into an
outline almost at once. In Lesson 2, MODNet only knew people. SAM cuts out anything you point at.

YOUR FILE
sticker_maker.py. Lines marked (given) are done for you: read their notes A to H. You write TODO 1 to TODO 5, in order.
The TODO comments explain each job in words. Run the program after each TODO and check the result.

STEP 1: Run the starter (setup: see SETUP.txt)
Point the camera at a toy, a bottle or a pencil case and press SPACE. The top line says "SAM is studying your photo..."
and never stops. Why? Read study().

STEP 2: TODO 1, study(): SAM part 1 studies the photo
Resize to 1024 x 1024, RGB, scale with MEAN and STD, reshape to (1, 3, 1024, 1024), run the encoder.
Check: after about a second the top line shows the click keys.

STEP 3: TODO 2, on_mouse(): your clicks are prompts
A click means "this", a right-click means "not this".
Check: a green dot where you click, a red dot where you right-click. The terminal says the guesses cover 0: that is TODO 3.

STEP 4: TODO 3, ask_sam(): SAM part 2 turns clicks into 3 guesses
Move each click to SAM's 1024 x 1024 size, then give SAM the points, the labels and no boxes.
Check: the numbers after "SAM's 3 guesses cover" are no longer 0.

STEP 5: TODO 4, pick(): choose a guess
Sort the 3 guesses from small to big, take one, and make it as big as the photo.
Check: the object turns yellow with a white edge. Press m: a part, then bigger, then the whole thing.

STEP 6: TODO 5: the see-through sticker
Add a 4th channel (alpha) from the outline and crop to the object.
Check: press s and open the sticker PNG in this folder: only the object, the background is see-through.

STEP 7: Find what the AI gets wrong
Try an object on a background of almost the same colour, two objects that touch, and something shiny or see-through
(a glass). When the outline misses a part, click that part too; when it takes too much, right-click the extra bit.

KEYS
SPACE = freeze the picture (SAM starts studying) / back to the live camera    click = this    right-click = not this
m = SAM's next guess (a part / bigger / the whole thing)    s = save the sticker    q or Esc = quit (or close the window)

TIPS
Hold the object still or put it on a table, and press SPACE when it is sharp. Good light helps. Each SPACE starts again
with no clicks. Errors appear in the terminal, not in the camera window.

SHOW YOUR PARENT
Make stickers of three different things, and say: "The AI was never told what these things are called. I point, and it
finds the outline."
