STICKER MAKER: SETUP

1. Keep this folder whole: sticker_maker.py and the 4 SAM files (vision_encoder_uint8.onnx, vision_encoder_uint8.onnx_data,
   prompt_encoder_mask_decoder_uint8.onnx, prompt_encoder_mask_decoder_uint8.onnx_data; 61 MB together) must stay together.
2. Open a terminal INSIDE this folder and run:
       python sticker_maker.py
   (on Mac or Linux you may need python3 instead of python). The first line of the program installs opencv-python,
   numpy and onnxruntime if they are missing (needs internet the first time). To install them yourself instead:
       python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
3. A window called "Sticker Maker" opens with your camera. Click on it once, so it gets your key presses.

Needs: Python 3.10 or newer, a webcam and a mouse or touchpad (right-click on a Mac touchpad: click with two fingers).
About 1 GB of free memory. No internet is needed after the install. Nothing you film leaves your computer.
If the window closes at once, or the top line says "SAM is studying your photo" for more than 10 seconds, read the
terminal: the error is there.
Camera busy or blocked? Close Zoom, Teams or the camera app. On Windows: Settings > Privacy > Camera, allow apps to use the camera.
